About this proof of concept
Human owner
Andrej Verity (UNOCHA)
Purpose
A proof-of-concept dashboard tracking public signals (GitHub, Hacker News, Google Trends, VS Code Marketplace) and news/video mentions for AI-assisted coding tools, to explore what an automated trend-tracking pipeline could look like. It is not meant to serve as a source of truth for product, investment, or strategic decisions.
Data sources
Figures are pulled from public APIs (GitHub, Hacker News/Algolia, a third-party Google Trends proxy, the VS Code Marketplace, and Product Hunt's "vibe-coding" category) - none are synthetic or estimated by design, though some third-party sources have known reliability gaps (see the notes on the Signals and Compare pages). News/video sources are configured in a Google Sheet maintained by hand, not in code. Full source and methodology: the project's GitHub repo.
AI involvement
This site's code, copy, and design were built with AI assistance (Claude). Tool pricing/platform facts in the comparison table are hand-curated, not AI-generated. Article/video relevance tagging optionally uses an LLM (Gemini) as a second opinion on top of keyword-based rules.
Limitations
Every signal here is a narrow proxy (e.g. GitHub topic-tag counts, comment volume on one forum), not a validated usage or market-share metric, and none are blended into a single score or ranking. Nothing on this site has been independently audited. Treat it as illustrative, not authoritative.
Growth and mention stats update automatically. Pricing and feature details are maintained by hand — verify against each vendor before making a purchase decision.
